## 39

HR 说这活儿钱少、事多、离家远。
我说这不巧了，三样我都不挑。
钱少，能锻炼我的欲望管理；
事多，能速成我的时间管理；
离家远，单程俩小时——
等于每天白送四小时思考人生，
还不用买票。

> HR said the pay's low, the workload's heavy, and the commute is two hours each way.
> I said perfect, I'm not picky about any of it.
> Low pay keeps my desire management sharp.
> Heavy workload fast-tracks my time management.
> Two hours each way? That's four hours of meditation a day,
> and I don't even need a ticket.
