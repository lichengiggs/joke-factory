## 65

朋友抱怨一百块不经花。我说你夸张。结果我自己点个外卖、打个车、买包纸巾——回头一看，它已经完成了今日份的光速离职。

> A friend complained that a hundred bucks never lasts. I said he was exaggerating. Then I ordered takeout, grabbed a ride, bought tissues—and looked back to find that hundred had already finished today's high-speed resignation.
