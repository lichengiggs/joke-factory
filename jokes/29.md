## 29

活早干完了。我不敢走，因为领导的灯还亮着。他走了我才敢走，这叫尊重。他熬夜是给老板看，我熬夜是给他看。昨晚他忽然回头：你在等我？我说：对，等您先谢幕。

> The work was done hours ago. I don't dare leave — the head of department's light is still on. He leaves, then I leave. That's respect. He stays late to impress the big boss. I stay late to impress him. Last night he turned around: "You waiting for me?" I said: "Yes. Waiting for you to take your bow."
