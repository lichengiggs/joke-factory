## 24

我盯着配送地图，看骑手在楼下绕了三圈。预计送达从六点改到六点半，又改到七点。我没催，我觉得他在做一件大事。最后门铃响了，饭凉了，他说了句对不起。我说没事，你辛苦了。他走了，我对着那盒饭说：欢迎回家。

> I watched the little rider icon circle my block three times. Six o'clock became six-thirty, then seven. I didn't nudge him. A man circling like that is clearly up to something important. Then the doorbell went. Cold food, one apology. I said, no worries, you worked hard. He left. I turned to the container and said: welcome home.
