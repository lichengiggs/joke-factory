## 64

连送外卖的都说，现在不知道哪家店干净。我问那你自己吃啥。他把餐盒往我手里一塞：我只管送到，开盖是另一单生意。

> Even the delivery guy said he doesn't know which places are clean anymore. I asked what he eats himself. He shoved the box into my hands: I only do door-to-door—opening the lid is a separate order.
