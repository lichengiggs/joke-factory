## 19

周一早会轮到我汇报，我把上周的原话念了一遍。
没人发现。
我顺手把上周的咳嗽也补上了。
领导点头：今天特别稳。
散会后，我听见整层楼都在咳嗽。

> Monday stand-up. My turn. I read last week's update word for word.
> Nobody noticed.
> So I threw in last week's cough.
> My boss nodded. "Steady as ever."
> By the afternoon, the whole floor was coughing.
