## 43

早高峰电梯，我和领导并排站着，全程没人说话。
我盯着楼层数字，数到七的时候，领导清了清嗓子。
我立刻把嘴角调成刚好能笑的弧度，准备接话。
结果他咳完就没了下文。
到十楼他先迈出去，我还站在原地，等他返场。

> Early-morning elevator, my boss and I stand side by side. Nobody says a word.
> I count the floor numbers. On seven, he clears his throat.
> I immediately dial my mouth into the exact smile that says "please continue."
> He coughs once and stops.
> On ten he steps out first. I stay put, waiting for the encore.
